01. The Problem

Small businesses are locked out of global trade

Over 80% of African small traders rely on third-party middlemen to source goods from China, suffering massive markups, fraudulent suppliers, and complex import regulations.

While enterprise importers possess dedicated foreign agents, small businesses face steep language barriers, opaque pricing, and lack access to verified supplier networks—leaving them unable to compete locally.

02. The Solution

Direct B2B trade infrastructure in your pocket

AFROCHINATRADE provides a unified digital pipeline that connects vetted Chinese manufacturers directly with African merchants, featuring real-time negotiation, logistics tracking, and verified merchant directories.

Built with a React Native mobile core and Node.js/Express backend, the platform manages end-to-end multi-tenant workflows, encrypted identity verification, and role-based access for buyers, suppliers, and logistics personnel.

Verified Supplier Network

Browse pre-screened Chinese factories categorized by industry with full business verification documentation.

Multi-Tenant Security

Role-based access control protecting trade credentials, invoices, and multi-user business accounts.

Direct Negotiation Hub

In-app messaging system allowing buyers to request custom quotes and settle order terms directly with factories.

Procurement Workflows

Track order stages from manufacturing through clearance and doorstep delivery in real time.

Smart Catalog Search

High-speed search engine optimized for low-bandwidth environments to find niche trade components quickly.

Trade Analytics

Built-in analytics dashboards giving merchants insights into sourcing costs, order timelines, and unit margins.

03. Business Impact

“When small businesses can source globally without gatekeepers, local economies thrive. AFROCHINATRADE turns cross-border trade into a streamlined, software-driven process.”

Published on Google Play, the platform serves as an operational backbone for active African traders, demonstrating how thoughtful full-stack engineering can solve large-scale international supply chain bottlenecks.
