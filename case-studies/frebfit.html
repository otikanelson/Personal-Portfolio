FREBFIT

Fitness trainers have expertise, but they lack a platform to reach clients beyond the gym. FREBFIT connects people with trainers, delivers personalized workouts, and tracks progress—all in one mobile app built to make fitness accessible.

GitHub

Built during NIIT internship

During my internship at NIIT, I met trainers struggling to connect with clients beyond the gym floor. They had the knowledge but lacked the tools. FREBFIT became the bridge—a mobile fitness app that helps people find trainers, select workouts, and track progress. My first real dive into native Android development taught me that code isn't just logic—it's about solving real human problems.

The Problem

Trainers have expertise, but no platform

Fitness trainers are stuck. They're limited to the physical walls of a gym, unable to scale their impact or reach clients who can't afford in-person sessions. Meanwhile, people looking to get fit often don't know where to start or who to trust.

The gym model works, but it's expensive, inflexible, and leaves too many people behind. Technology should fix this gap, not widen it.

The Solution

Connect, train, track—anywhere

FREBFIT is a native Android fitness app built with Java and Android Studio. Users browse trainer profiles, select workout programs, follow guided exercises, and track their fitness progress over time. Trainers get a platform to reach more clients. Users get affordable, flexible fitness guidance.

Material Design principles ensure the interface feels intuitive. Every feature is designed to get people moving, not scrolling.

Trainer Profiles

Browse certified trainers, read reviews, and select the right fit for your goals.

Workout Programs

Pre-built workout plans tailored for different fitness levels and goals.

Guided Exercises

Step-by-step instructions with animations to ensure proper form and technique.

Progress Tracking

Log workouts, track reps and weights, and visualize fitness improvements over time.

Native Android

Built with Java and Android Studio for smooth performance and reliable functionality.

Material Design

Clean, intuitive interface following Android design guidelines for familiarity.

Philosophy
Code isn't just logic—it's about solving real human problems. FREBFIT was my first lesson in building software that actually matters to people.

FREBFIT was more than a project—it was proof that technology can democratize access to fitness expertise. When trainers can reach more people and users can train anywhere, everyone gets stronger.
