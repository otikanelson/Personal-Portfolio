Case Study — Desktop Storyboarding Tool

SCENORY

A fast, distraction-free desktop environment for visual storytellers. Scenory gives animators, short-film directors, and comic artists an infinite canvas to block out shots, layer drawings, and export animatics — without switching between ten different apps.

Who it's for

Built for people who tell stories in pictures

Scenory doesn't try to be a general-purpose creative suite. It's built around three specific workflows, each with its own shorthand for planning a sequence before committing it to film, frame, or page.

Animators

Keyframe key beats directly into timeline sequences, preview movement speed, and sync pencil-test timing without leaving the board.

Short films

Plan camera angles, focal lengths, and scene flow before hitting set. Block out actor movement and communicate the shot instantly.

Comics

Draft page layouts, gutters, and speech-bubble guides on screen, then export multi-page spreads as high-resolution vector lines.

How it works

Three ideas the whole tool is built around

01

Infinite canvas, instant pacing

Boards live on a canvas with no page breaks to plan around. Arrange sequence thumbnails freely, reorder beats with a drag, and tweak timing on the fly while script notes and annotations stay attached to the shot they belong to.

02

Non-destructive layered drawing

Foreground details, actor passes, and environment backgrounds sit on separate layers, so a rough pass never has to overwrite a clean one. Fluid pen controls keep sketching fast without sacrificing the ability to undo one layer at a time.

03

One-click animatic export

Board sequences convert straight into a video preview, complete with camera pans, zooms, and audio cues — so a rough cut is ready to review without opening a separate editor.

Early feedback
