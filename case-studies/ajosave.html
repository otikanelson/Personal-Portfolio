01. Cultural Inspiration

How Africa has always saved together

For generations, millions of people across West Africa have relied on communal rotating savings: Ajo, Esusu, and Adashe. Members of a trusted group contribute a fixed amount regularly, taking turns receiving the entire pooled pot. It provides working capital, micro-loans, and structured savings without complex banking barriers.

The underlying issue? Paper-based vulnerabilities. Manual notebooks get lost, payments are forgotten, and administrative overhead strains social trust. The economic model is brilliant—it simply needed modern fintech infrastructure.

02. Engineering & Solution

Preserving tradition with production-grade engineering

AjoSave digitizes rotation logistics through a unified Monorepo setup, bridging a React Native mobile client with a responsive web portal. Secured with OTP phone authentication and backed by clear ledger schemas, every member knows exactly when their payout arrives.

Custom Thrift Groups

Define custom contribution cycles (daily, weekly, monthly), maximum group capacity, and payout rotation rules.

Digital Wallet Engine

In-app wallet funding and transaction logs ensure every deposit is recorded with cryptographic integrity.

Transparent Ledger

Real-time auditability. Every contribution and payout is visible to group members to enforce trust.

OTP Verification

Low-friction authentication using phone numbers, making onboarding simple for all technical skill levels.

Smart Reminders

Automated push notifications and SMS alerts ensure missed payment cycles are virtually eliminated.

Monorepo Scalability

Architected as a unified codebase shared across web and mobile platforms for seamless feature deployment.

03. Philosophy & Product Vision

“The best financial tools don't replace cultural systems—they empower them. AjoSave proves technology can honor tradition while giving communities the tools to build wealth.”

Developed for Codefest Africa 2025, AjoSave stands out by combining grassroots socio-economic habits with scalable fintech design. It delivers real-world utility for traders, student circles, and community cooperatives.
