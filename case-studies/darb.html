DARB

Great ideas die because funding is too complicated. DARB makes crowdfunding simple—creators launch campaigns, supporters contribute with a click, and everyone sees exactly where the money goes.

View Live Site → GitHub
The Problem

Good ideas shouldn't die because funding is hard

Crowdfunding platforms exist, but they're either too complicated, take too much commission, or hide where the money actually goes. Creators deserve transparency. Supporters deserve accountability.

The barrier shouldn't be the platform—it should be the idea itself.

The Solution

Simple campaigns, transparent funding

DARB is a full-stack crowdfunding platform where creators launch campaigns, set funding goals, and supporters contribute directly. Campaign management, contribution tracking, secure payments, and data visualization—all built with React.js, Tailwind CSS, Vite, and Node.js.

No middlemen. No hidden fees. Just transparent funding that gives good ideas a real shot at becoming reality.

Campaign Creation

Launch funding campaigns with goals, timelines, and detailed project descriptions.

Progress Tracking

Real-time funding progress with visual dashboards showing contributions and milestones.

Secure Payments

Integrated payment processing with transaction verification and receipts.

Supporter Management

Track contributors, send updates, and maintain communication with your backers.

Transparency

Every contribution is visible. Every expense is tracked. No hidden fees or unclear allocations.

Data Visualization

Interactive dashboards showing funding trends, contributor demographics, and campaign performance.

Philosophy
Funding shouldn't be a barrier to building something meaningful. When the platform gets out of the way, ideas can speak for themselves.

DARB is live and functional—a working crowdfunding platform built to prove that transparency and simplicity don't have to be mutually exclusive.
